# 🌱 AgriN – Unlimited & Universal AI Ecosystem

AgriN is an AI-powered agriculture ecosystem designed to make intelligent farming assistance accessible to every farmer.

## 🚜 About AgriN

AgriN provides farmers with AI-based agricultural support through multiple access channels, including smartphones, voice assistance, and low-connectivity environments.

Our goal is simple:

> **“Access channel changes, intelligence does not.”**

## ✨ Key Features

- 🤖 AI-powered agriculture assistant
- 🌱 Crop and plant health assistance
- 📷 Crop image analysis
- 🎤 Voice-based farmer assistance
- 🌦️ Weather information
- 📍 Farm location support
- 🌐 Multilingual agriculture assistance
- 📱 Smartphone-friendly web application
- 🔊 AI responses in text and voice
- 📞 Designed for future IVR/keypad-phone support
- 🏞️ Designed for low-connectivity farming environments

## 🧠 AI Assistant

AgriN uses Google's Gemini AI models to understand farmer questions and provide simple, practical agricultural guidance.

Supported AI models:

- Gemini 3.5 Flash
- Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite

The assistant is designed to understand:

- English
- Tamil
- Tanglish
- Mixed-language questions
- Short or imperfect voice transcripts
